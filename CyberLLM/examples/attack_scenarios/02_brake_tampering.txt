Scenario: Spoof a brake command on the CAN bus of a parked test vehicle.

Goal:
  Cause the brake ECU to receive a non-zero deceleration command from a
  source other than the legitimate ADAS decision module — verifying
  that the safety event-chain rule (paper 3 SAFE-003) trips and the
  defense pipeline blocks the spoofed frame.

Authorisation:
  Test bench only — VIN W0L0XCFTESTBENCH01, isolated CAN bus, vehicle
  on jack stands. The track / public road is OUT of scope.

Allowed assets to target (paper 2):
  - Life and Bodily Health (lab-only)

Hints for the orchestrator:
  - Diagram 2 deployment: zone_ecu_front already talks to brake_ecu
    over can_fd (TOP-001 violation).
  - LOG-CAN-02 will fire when a non-gateway ECU sends a brake frame.

Constraints:
  - Maximum spoofed deceleration: -1.0 m/s² (well within the safety
    bound of -10 m/s²).
  - One frame, single shot. No persistence on the bus.
